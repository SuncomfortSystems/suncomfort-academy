About us

South London's trusted renewable energy team

SunComfort Systems was founded to make renewable energy accessible, reliable, and straightforward for homeowners and businesses across London and the South East.

Based in Elephant and Castle, our team of 35 includes heat pump specialists, solar engineers, electrical technicians, and a dedicated maintenance division — all operating from one base with 18 fully-equipped service vehicles.
