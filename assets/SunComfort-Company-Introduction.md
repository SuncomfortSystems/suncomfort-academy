# SunComfort Systems — Company Introduction

## About the Company

SunComfort Systems is an independent renewable energy and power services company serving residential and light commercial properties. Founded in 2012, the company has grown from a two-person solar installation team to a 35-employee operation.

## Core Services

| Service | Revenue Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Solar | 50% | Flagship service — UK and US |
| Heat Pumps | 20% | Primarily UK market |
| Power / Generators | 20% | Primarily US market |
| General Maintenance | 10% | Incidental on-site work |

Electrical work is embedded across all services as a supporting trade, not a standalone revenue line. General Maintenance covers incidental remedial work (plastering, minor plumbing, minor electrical) completed alongside primary service visits.

## Common Job Scenarios

- Solar annual service → engineer notices roof flashing deteriorating → general maintenance job raised and scheduled
- Generator maintenance (commercial) → customer asks about backup battery → power + battery storage quote raised
- Heat pump survey (residential) → heat loss calculation identifies undersized radiators → radiator upgrade scoped before installation proceeds
- Solar PV installation (commercial) → consumer unit too old to connect safely → variation deflected to office for electrical upgrade quote
- Annual solar service → panels heavily soiled and underperforming → panel clean variation raised on site
- Generator callout (commercial) → transfer switch fault discovered → defect raised, parts ordered, follow-up job scheduled
